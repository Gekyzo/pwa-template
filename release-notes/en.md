# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- **Delete all data** in Settings removes the notes and the preferences from this device, and the app starts as new.

## 0.1.3 - 2026-10-25

- You sign in with your Google account, or tap **Use offline** to use the app with no account. The administrator approves each new account. Your notes stay on this device.

## 0.1.2 - 2026-10-24

- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
